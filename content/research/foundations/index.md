<div class="pp-topic-intro">
<p>My research investigates the mathematical principles underlying learning and representation. Drawing on information theory and statistical decision theory, I study how representations retain task-relevant information, how their usefulness can be compared, and how knowledge can be transferred across models.</p>
<p>The objective is to connect the design of learning systems with precise questions about information, sufficiency, and downstream decisions.</p>
</div>
<section class="pp-topic-questions" aria-labelledby="key-questions">
<h2 id="key-questions">Key questions</h2>
<ul>
<li>What makes one representation more useful than another?</li>
<li>How can information-theoretic principles guide learning objectives?</li>
<li>How can multiple models contribute to reusable representations?</li>
</ul>
</section>
